I study how machine learning can extract reliable clinical information from imperfect medical records.

My main project reads smartphone photographs of paper 12-lead ECGs. I collected 1,191 photographs from 1,175 patients at a teaching hospital in Dhaka, built a pre-specified evaluation protocol, and tested a plain image model on a locked test set opened once (macro AUROC 0.923, 95% CI 0.895-0.946), on nine physical forms of public ECGs (scanned, crumpled, stained, on a monitor), and on external data from Germany, Pakistan and the USA.

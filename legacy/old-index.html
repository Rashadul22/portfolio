BSc Thesis · Ongoing

Multimodal CVD Diagnosis from Smartphone-Captured Paper ECGs

Trimodal late fusion — ConvNeXt-Small CNN + XGBoost-ECG + XGBoost-Clinical — for five-class multi-label CVD classification. Original dataset of 1,191 patients at Enam Medical College, Dhaka. First multimodal smartphone-ECG dataset from Bangladesh. Cross-nationally validated without retraining (Pakistan CPEIC + NHFB Bangladesh).

First AI/ML Analysis of NASA OSD-970 — ISS Microgravity Thermogenesis

First published ML re-analysis of NASA OSDR dataset OSD-970 (RR-1 ISS mission). RT-qPCR data, 89 genes, 16 female C57BL/6J mice, 37 days microgravity. Revealed 12.21-fold Ucp1 upregulation in WAT. Identified the Angpt2–Jun–Irs2–Klf2 axis via SHAP. Relevant to female astronaut health and obesity research.

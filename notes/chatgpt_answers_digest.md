# ChatGPT answers: digest (what we adopt, what we reject)

## Prompt 3, professor view (received 24 Nov 2026, two answers)
Adopted:
- First screen answers 5 questions with no scrolling or interaction: who, what problem, what I did, where is the
  evidence, what position I seek. No loading screen, no intro animation.
- Tagline: "Medical AI beyond clean digital data." + positioning sentence; "Seeking RA, MSc and PhD positions" line.
- AUROC never alone: always "locked test, 95% CI"; never "accuracy %"; no counters.
- Robustness wording: give the range and that the same ECGs were compared (paired), not "unaffected by damage".
- Outputs hierarchy: Peer-reviewed / Preprints ("not peer reviewed") / Submitted / In preparation (only with a real
  draft) / Thesis (supervisor named). Each item has a one-line "My role".
- Each research page: question, motivation, data and method, findings with uncertainty, MY CONTRIBUTION (mandatory),
  limitations and outputs.
- NASA: "Independent secondary analysis of public NASA OSDR data"; no NASA logo, no "NASA researcher". The OSDR
  AI/ML working-group membership is real (CV) and stays in Credentials, worded exactly.
- Download CV button is expected (we still need the PDF from the user).
- Professors' likely questions to answer on the ECG page: remarks masked from inputs (yes, band-masked), patient-level
  split (yes), protocol fixed before test (yes), external models frozen (yes), ethics.
Rejected:
- "To our knowledge, one of the first..." (project rule: never "first").
- Placeholder titles it invented for papers (use real titles/working descriptions only).
